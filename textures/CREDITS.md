# Planet textures

The planet surface maps in this folder come from [Solar System Scope](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/)
and are used under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
They were downscaled (to 256–1024 px wide) for the home page's 3D planets.
